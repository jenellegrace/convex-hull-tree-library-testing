Convex hull geometries approximate each species' mature canopy form for use in shading and solar radiation calculations within the Rhino/Grasshopper heat stress model. Height, canopy diameter, crown shape, and mesh statistics shown here are representative placeholder values pending export from the production hull-generation definition — see the About page for details. Ground-plane, facade, and foliage material reflectance data used alongside these hulls is maintained in the companion Spectral Materials Reference.
