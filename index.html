Convex Hull Tree Library

about materials reference ↗

Convex hull geometries approximate each species' mature canopy form for use in shading and solar radiation calculations within the Rhino/Grasshopper heat stress model. Height, canopy width/depth, hull volume, and mesh statistics shown here are computed directly from each uploaded 3D model — species with no model uploaded yet are marked as such rather than showing an estimated number. See the About page for details. Ground-plane, facade, and foliage material reflectance data used alongside these hulls is maintained in the companion Spectral Materials Reference.
